01 / ABOUT

Purpose, voice
& impact.

I’m Bukola Ayeni, a Nigerian student and emerging changemaker passionate about using my voice, creativity and community work to contribute to a better world.

I am a University of Abuja student studying Botany, while building experience across public speaking, youth advocacy, volunteering, writing, leadership and social impact. My interests sit at the intersection of health, education, gender equality, youth development and the Sustainable Development Goals.

I believe young people should not only be spoken about — we should be part of the conversations, rooms and solutions shaping our communities.

04 / QUEENS UNITE INITIATIVE

Ending period poverty.
Empowering girls.

Queens Unite Initiative is my youth-led platform creating space for girls and young women to learn, grow, speak up and feel supported.

Our work includes menstrual health and hygiene education, confidence and body-image conversations, mental health awareness, leadership and girl-child rights.
